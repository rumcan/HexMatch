Hexmatch Industries — third-party notices
=========================================

This file ships with the game build. It replaces the credit line that used to
sit at the foot of the main menu.

ART
---
The buildings, industries, depots, roads, vehicles and ground the game draws
are original Hexmatch art.

The build still contains an older sprite atlas and the road/building layer
sheets cut from it (assets/atlas@*.png, assets/roads@*.png,
assets/buildings@*.png). Those files are derived from OpenGFX:
