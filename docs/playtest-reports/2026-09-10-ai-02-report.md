# AI-02 — the parked rival, paved-truck speed, a 20★ race, and difficulty at boot

**Date:** 2026-09-10 (player feedback round 2, follows AI-01 / PR #61)
**Build:** `arena/01a08aba-hexmatch` (post-360cf27), dev preview
**Harnesses:** a headless LIVE game (`tests/unit/zz-live.test.ts`, scratch —
drives `econTick`/`aiTick` at 1s steps over the real boot), plus the AI-01
race sim with `VP_TARGET` re-derived everywhere.

## What the player reported (after watching a long session)

1. *"The rival has one factory and one road and one depot and it is just
   upgrading it"* — no more expansion; *"if I do nothing, the AI does
   nothing."*
2. *"It is not playing the match game to gain gold, and using the Black
   Market on me?"* — zero raids, ever.
3. Trucks should be 2× as fast on paved road as on dirt.
4. *"Increase win points to 20. 10 is way too little."*
5. *"Make the player select the difficulty at the start of the game."*

## 1–2. Diagnosis — a real deadlock, not a lazy clock

Repro inside the real game (seed 1337, default rival, thinnest possible human
setup, then no further input), driven at the game's own clocks for 25 in-game
minutes:

| clock | depots | tiles | paved | ★ | purse |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T+1m | 1 | 3 | 2 | 0.5 | stone 4, ore 3, wood 2 |
| T+6m | 1 | 3 | 2 | 0.5 | **wood 158** |
| T+25m | 1 | 3 | 2 | 0.5 | **wood 766** |

The seat thought (one turn every 9s), earned wood at +32/min, and never built
again. The race sim never produced this — its lanes happened to earn Stone,
so its bank pipes stayed open — which is why AI-01's calibration missed it.

**Mechanism — three guards agreeing on the same wrong number.** The rival
keeps a Plant's worth of Ore in reserve when a plant is "wanted"
(`planUpgrades`' keepOre, gate AI-01 added). On the repro lane it held
exactly the Ore its one remaining gravel tile needed (4) with a 3-Ore plant
reserve — so the pave pass could never legally pay (`4−3 < 4`). Meanwhile
every affordability check measured the goal against the RAW purse:
`paveMilestone`'s skint-gap was 0 → `rivalBankTowardPlan` thought "nothing
to do" every turn, and `rivalBankTowardPave` agreed. No bank, no pave, no
depot, forever — with banks that traded exactly 0 units in 25 minutes. (The
"AI does nothing when I idle" reading is partially also trivially true: the
rival cannot build during the player's setup phase, by design.)

**The raids were structurally impossible.** Lorry deliveries fed only the
player's match-3 board ("the rival's lorries feed no board"), so the rival
earned cargo only via industry trickle and ZERO Gold — and `rivalRaid`'s
first guard is "no Gold, no raid".

## Fixes, and what they measure afterwards

**Pave goals are priced against SPENDABLE Ore** (purse minus the plant
reserve) in all three places (`spendableOre()` in `src/iso/game.ts`), and
the bank pointed at a pave goal buys Ore *past* the reserve instead of
stopping one trade short. Same lane, same rule-set, afterwards:

| clock | depots | tiles | paved | ★ | note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T+2m | 2 | 9 | 4 | 1 | bank bought Stone/Oil first |
| T+6m | 4 | 32 | 19 | 4.75 | — |
| T+11m | 5 | 69 | 40 | **10** | the old 10★ line — the rival beat an idle player |

This exact scenario is now a regression test: "AI-02 the rival keeps playing
for minutes (the live stall)" in `iso-game.test.ts` asserts ≥4 depots, ≥12
paves, and no 300+ Wood hoard after six in-game minutes with no gifts.

**Rival lorries pay the rival.** A delivered load credits the seat that owns
the truck: +1 per cargo plus +1 Gold (the abstract of what a moderately
played board pays). Gold floats print over the rival's Factory so the income
is visible. Consequence measured on seed 1337 vs hard, 22 minutes: Gold
climbs 0→6 by T+10m and fires **Blockades on the player's busiest industry
about once a minute from T+10m until the rival crosses the line at T+16.5m**
(6 depots, 86 tiles, 80 paved, 20★ — vs an idle player). `rivalRaid`'s other
cards (fog/girders/frost) fire whenever their price is reached.

**Trucks run 2× on paved segments.** The triangle-fold uniform-speed model
couldn't express mixed paces, so `tickTrucks` now integrates segment by
segment (`segFast` computed per route segment at every replan; paved if
either endpoint's tile carries a paved tier — public/town roads included).
A mixed lane pays Σ(len·(paved ? 150 : 300)ms) instead of a flat fade; the
home turn still counts the delivery exactly once. Refolding at both ends
remains exact for large ticks; trucks without `segFast` (old saves) drive
the old uniform pace. Paved-versus-dirt is pinned in
`tests/unit/iso-vehicles-speed.test.ts`.

**The line is 20★.** `VICTORY.target` is the single source — the HUD
"/N", help modal, banners, win-check all derive from it; the AI-01
ship-constant tests were re-numbered inline with the change (40 paves → 80,
etc).

**Difficulty is chosen at boot.** When nothing has chosen yet — no
`?rival=` URL and nothing valid in localStorage — the game opens with a
three-card chooser (label + blurb + pace line, current resolver default
marked), pick applies to the live game, persists for next boot, and syncs
the top-bar selector (which stays for mid-game changes). A corrupted stored
value cannot silence the prompt: the gate parses exactly like the resolver.

## Calibration at the 20★ line (seed 1337, 40-minute windows)

Mirror matches (each preset vs itself):

| preset | finish | trailer at finish | offers posted/taken |
|---|---|---|---|
| hard | 15.5m | 7★ | 10 / 0 |
| normal | 16.8m | 8★ | 13 / 1 |
| easy | 28.5m | 18.5★ | 26 / 10 |

Head-to-head (both chairs):

| pairing | result |
|---|---|
| hard (you) vs easy (ai) | hard 20★ @15.5m, easy 7★ — hard wins |
| easy (you) vs hard (ai) | easy 17.25★, hard **20.75★** @27.5m — hard wins |
| easy (you) vs normal (ai) | easy 17.25★, normal **20.5★** @27.3m — normal wins |

Ladder holds by the user's own measure (speed to the win line per chair):
hard < normal < easy, hard beats easy from BOTH chairs. The AI-01 trailer
floor's 20-minute nominal was the 10★ era's; at 20★ it is recalibrated to 30
minutes (measured game lengths 15.5–28.5m — sub-linear, since lanes
compound). Race harness milestones read `VP_TARGET`, so the ladder's
assertions scale with the line by construction.

## Acceptance mapping

- Rival expands in live play (not one-depot-and-stuck): repro + regression
  test, measured above. `aiTick` remains frame-clocked — the stall was
  affording, not thinking.
- "Plays match-3 for gold and raids": delivery income + verified blockade
  purchases in the 22-minute live run.
- Trucks 2× on paved: shipped per segment, tests pinned.
- Win points 20: shipped; race/calibration re-measured and re-passing.
- Difficulty at game start: boot prompt with URL/storage escapes, 9 unit
  tests (`iso-skill-picker.test.ts`); top-bar selector unchanged.

Deferred on purpose: the race sim does not model lorry delivery income (both
seats would gain it symmetrically — it is a live-experience feature, and the
ladder it calibrates is pace-driven). Revisit if a future lane shows the
sim/live pace gap widening.
