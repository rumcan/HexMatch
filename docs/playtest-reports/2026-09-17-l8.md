---

## The four steps, as the HUD hands them over

**1. Connect — the objective line says the goal, in the loop's order.**
A bare boot reads *"Place your Factory beside a town — it anchors your whole
network."* Finish setup and the line moves on by itself: with no Depot it asks for
one, with a Depot and no route it says which half is missing (*"…road it in"*),
and once the network runs it asks for the next rung or the city. It is **one line**
over the map, never a menu, and it hides only while a real banner (a protest
countdown, a disconnect, the ending) owns that lane.
→ `01-objective-setup.jpg`, `02-objective-connected.jpg`

**2. Tune — the board opens saying what it is for, and closes when it is done.**
Placing the Depot opens the match-3 session immediately, and the objective line
becomes *"Match to set your Stone Depot's output — 7/10 moves left."* The plate
prints the session's own numbers and the yield it is building toward; Finish
commits it, ✕ pays the floor. Nothing else on the screen asks for a decision while
the board is up.

**3. Earn — the number on the chip is the number the clock pays.**
Every cargo chip carries its live rate (`+0.3 /s` on grain in the shot). Three
econ ticks on a connected, tuned Depot bank `rate × 9 s`, within the clock's own
fractional carry — asserted in `tests/unit/iso-l8-legibility.test.ts` and again in
`tests/e2e/iso-l8-legibility.spec.ts`, so the readout cannot drift from the purse.
Hovering the Depot prints the factors the tick multiplies: `yield: ×1 · transport:
dirt ×1`, `distance: 4 tiles · ×1 (near)`, `rate: 1/tick · 0.3/s 🌾 Grain`, plus
`decay:` on the Hard chair only. **Every gain has a visible cause**, and a stopped
one says so instead of promising income (`rate: no route — not ticking`,
`· protest — stopped`).
→ `03-depot-inspector.jpg`

**4. Spend — the build list quotes, the rungs open, the city lifts the rates.**
Prices are on the buttons (`2🪵 2🪨` for a plant), the Depot button quotes the type
the tile would raise, and buying the city tier visibly lifts every connected
Depot's `/s` — the spend half is the same readout the earn half uses.

## The optional quests (the ticket's addition)

Under the objective line sits **one slim line**: `⚑ Quests 3 ▸`. Opening it lists
2–3 suggestions, **one per strategy** — claim ground, connect Depots, tune deeper,
raise the city, broaden the cargo mix — each in a character's voice (the sandbox's
neutral foreman; a contract is voiced by its cast, rival for the aggressive plans
and the guide for the how-to ones). Every row carries its progress (`1/2`,
`×1.0 of ×2`) and what finishing pays (`2⛏️ Ore`), a **✕** retires that one for
good, and **Hide quests** puts the panel away — both remembered across a reload.

Three rules the panel keeps, all checked in tests:

* **Nothing gates.** No depot tier, upgrade, rung, ★ total or win condition reads
  the panel. Dismiss all three, hide the panel, win the match anyway — that is a
  legal game, and the acceptance line says so.
* **The map decides.** The claim quest names a cargo that is actually still
  unclaimed (contested ground first), the network plan's ask grows with what the
  seat already runs, so two seeds offer different plans.
* **Dismissing is an answer, not a nag.** A dismissal refills nothing; the panel
  draws again only when the seat actually moves (a new Depot, a new cargo on the
  clock, a rung, a city tier) or when a quest was just paid.

→ `04-quests-open.jpg`

## What is deliberately NOT in this ticket

Per the issue's own MVP scope, and unchanged here: the **tutorial rewrite (#230)**
(the tour still speaks the shipped loop's sentences), **quest ★** (L13/#228 owns

1–2 units, once), and the **decay display outside the inspector** (the Hard row's
`decay:` line is in the Depot card; the rest of the difficulty surfacing is #220's).
