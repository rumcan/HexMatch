## 1. The bug: a wire with one end

`Board.onFx` has existed since the board was restored (`src/game/board.ts`) and
`ui.fx` / `ui.popup` have existed since the HUD was restored
(`src/game/ui.ts`). **Nothing ever connected them.** `createQuarry` wires
`onHarvest`, `onGold`, `onPopup` and `onChange`; `createOriginalUi` is called
afterwards and no line anywhere assigned `onFx`. So every `pop`, `crack`,
`up`, `boom`, `bad` and `chain` event died on the board — including COMBO,
CHAIN and MATCH 5.

Four more defects were hiding behind that one missing line:

| # | Defect | Fix |
|---|--------|-----|
| 1 | `onFx` never assigned → all FX dead | assigned in `game.ts` right after `createOriginalUi` |
| 2 | callout text only fired at `chain >= 2` → a plain 3-match cleared in silence | `arcadeLabel()`: every pass speaks, chain 1 included |
| 3 | `CHAIN xN` fired **after** `gravity` + 400 ms of sleeps — it described a match already swept off the board | the callout fires inside `resolve()`, before the pause and before gravity |
| 4 | a tokenless match has an empty `gains`, so `onPopup` never fired and its COMBO label was lost | `onPopup` fires on the **label** as well as the gains |
| 5 | `prefers-reduced-motion` sets `animation-duration: .01ms !important`, which lands every `forwards` FX on its last keyframe (`opacity: 0`) — reduced-motion players saw **nothing** | the text readouts are exempted (static but readable); decorative pops still collapse |

### The words

`arcadeLabel(chain)` in `src/game/board.ts` — one callout per cascade pass, at
the centre of the group the player just matched:

| cascade step | text | type | board-wide float |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `MATCH!` | `chain` | yes (small) |
| 2 | `COMBO x2` | `combo` | yes (`.cf-big`) |
| 3+ | `CHAIN x3!!` | `combo` | yes (`.cf-big`) |
| a match-5 / L | `MATCH 5` / `L-SHAPE` | `chain` | yes |
| a match-5 deep in a cascade | `MATCH 5 · COMBO x2` | `combo` | yes (`.cf-big`) |

The float is **replaced, not stacked**: a cascade reads as one banner that
re-words itself and grows (MATCH! → COMBO x2 → CHAIN x3!!) instead of a column
of overlapping text.

## 2. What the DOM really contains

Booted in jsdom, one plain 3-match, then a two-deep cascade:

```html
<!-- three cleared gems, one flash each, at their cells -->
<div class="fx fx-pop"  style="left:  40px; top: 40px;"></div>
<div class="fx fx-pop"  style="left: 120px; top: 40px;"></div>
<div class="fx fx-pop"  style="left: 200px; top: 40px;"></div>
<!-- the callout, at the centre of the matched group -->
<div class="fx fx-chain" style="left: 120px; top: 40px;">MATCH!</div>
<div class="combo-float">MATCH!</div>

<!-- the next pass replaces the float rather than stacking a second one -->
<div class="combo-float cf-big">COMBO x2</div>
<div class="fx fx-combo" style="left: 120px; top: 120px;">COMBO x2</div>
<!-- a tokenless cascade: label only, no empty body -->
<div class="harvest-pop"><b class="hp-label">COMBO x2</b></div>
```

### A truthfulness bug found on the way

The readout used to show the token's **face value** while the purse banked
double (a depot-fed token pays `amount * 2`). Now invisible, it would have
been visible and wrong. `onHarvest` may return the amount actually credited,
and the board accumulates exactly that, so the floating `+2 🌾` and the purse
agree.

## 3. The lorry IS the delivery

Token spawns used to come from a 20 s clock with a per-tile surcharge
(`SPAWN_BASE_MS + distance × SPAWN_PER_TILE_MS`) that knew nothing about the
road it was pretending to model: the number on the gem and the lorry on the
map were two unrelated systems describing one cargo.

Now a lorry reaching the Factory **is** the delivery:

* `Truck` carries `depotId`, `factory` and `deliveries`; `tickTrucks` counts
  one delivery each time the truck turns round at the Factory end.
* The frame loop diffs `deliveries` against what it last saw and, per arrival,
  calls `quarry.deliver(cargo)` — which mints the token **and returns the tier
  it minted**.
* The `+N` float pops over the Factory tile only when a token actually landed
  (`deliver` returns 0 for a cut line or a board with no plain gem of that
  colour left). The number on the map and the token on the board cannot
  disagree.
* RV-03's intent survives and is now literal: distance costs real travel time
  — a far depot waits `2 × route × 300 ms` per load. The flat base clock is
  left only for cargo **no lorry carries** (rail-only, or a depot with no road
  route), which `setTruckServed` tells the quarry about so nothing is paid
  twice.

Cadence note: a lorry now delivers roughly every `2 × route × 300 ms` — on a
6-tile route that is ~3 s, against the old ~32 s. That is the deliberate
consequence of "1:1 with the truck" and the most likely thing to want toning
down (a per-cargo cooldown at `quarry.deliver` is the one-line knob).

## 4. Black Market sabotage lands on the rival

`Frost Tiles`, `Iron Girders` and `Smog Cloud` called straight into
`quarry.board` — the buyer's **own** Processing Plant. (`Blockade` was always
correct: it auto-picks the rival's busiest industry.)

The rival had nowhere else for them to go — `game.ts` says so outright: *"the
rival has no board to play, so the passive yield stays as its income."* So the
fix is two halves, and the second is the one that matters:

1. **The rival gets a plant** — `src/iso/rival-plant.ts`, a real `Board`.
2. **It has to be worth hitting.** The plant's state scales the rival's trickle
   income: `health()` = `1 − (ice + 2 × girders) / 56`, halved again by smog,
   floored at 10%. Seven ice tiles cost 13% of the rival's income; ice + girders
   + smog costs ~63%. Every effect expires (45 s / 60 s / 30 s) because ice on a
   board nobody plays would otherwise be permanent.

Marker: a float over the rival's Factory (`❄ 7 FROZEN`, `🏗 4 GIRDERS`,
`🌫 SMOG`) plus a toast naming the damage.

### Consequence, and what was done about it

Nothing could dirty the player's board any more, which left **Repair Crew**
with nothing to repair and **Security Forces** with nothing to guard. The
Black Market is a two-sided shop, so the rival now buys back: on `RAID_EVERY`
it buys one sabotage it can afford, lands it on the *player's* board, and is
turned away by Security Forces (which finally do what their description says).
Without this, two purchases in the panel are dead weight.

**Open question for the owner:** rival raids were not requested. They are the
only thing keeping Repair Crew and Security honest, but if the asymmetry is
wanted instead, delete `rivalRaid` and disable those two buttons.
