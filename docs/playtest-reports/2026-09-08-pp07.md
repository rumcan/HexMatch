# PP-07 — Catan-style rebalance: simulated playtest on the real 144×144 map

**Status: implemented.** Costs now come from one authoritative table
(`BUILD_COSTS` in `src/iso/config.ts`); the opening was replayed headlessly
on real generated maps (seeds 1337, 7, 2024) through the same planning and
pricing code the game runs. The simulation lives in
`tests/unit/iso-pp07-costs.test.ts` (section 3), so the numbers below
re-measure on every suite run instead of rotting.

## The ticket's proposal, as implemented

| Purchase | Cost |
|---|---|
| Road tile | 1 Wood + 1 Stone |
| Rail tile | 1 Wood + 1 Stone + 4 Ore |
| Upgrade Road to Rail | 4 Ore |
| Additional Depot | 1 Wood + 1 Stone + 1 Grain + 1 Oil |
| Additional Processing Plant | 2 Wood + 2 Stone + 2 Grain + 3 Ore |

Starting stock retuned to `{ wood: 12, stone: 12 }` (was `{ stone: 12 }`):
one paid road tile still costs one unit of starting stock per cargo, so the
E8 opening curve — 12 free tiles + 12 paid tiles — is unchanged in shape.
Ore, grain and oil stay unearned at boot: rail is still gated behind an ore
mine (E8/W9), depot expansion behind grain + oil, the second plant behind
grain + ore. Gold buys nothing here (PP-08: sabotage only).

No processing-yield change was needed: a tier-1 token in an ordinary match
pays exactly 1 (pinned per cargo in the new suite), so every ticket above is
1–4 token matches per cargo — costs and yields already agree. This was
verified by the progression runs below, not assumed.

## Method

Per seed: `generateMap(seed)` → factory sited by the game's own
`chooseRivalFactorySpot` probe (a tile with a verified-viable opening plan)
→ `aiBuildStep` for the free depot + road connection (1 AI tick = 9s game
time) → then processing income until the second depot and second plant are
affordable. Income model is the floor, not the ceiling: +1 per cargo the
network reaches (one tier-1 token match) plus ONE manufactured match-4 of
the most-missing cargo per round (PP-04's rule — currently landing on a
parallel ticket; the board-level forge it depends on is pinned per colour
in the suite). Every +1 is one token the player had to match, so "matches"
is the honest currency; the seconds assume the 20s token cadence as an
upper bound — a player matching several tokens per board is faster.

(Seed 1337's 24-tile connection spends the full starting stock — the bound
the suite asserts is `FREE_TRACK + 12`, i.e. the opening can never strand
the player past what the stock pays. A human placing factory and depot
adjacently connects shorter and cheaper than these probed worst cases.)

## No endless dependency loop

Every priced cargo is either in the starting stock (wood, stone) or produced
by a quota'd industry on every map — and every cargo has a gem colour, so a
missing unit is always one depot placement or one forged match-4 away, never
behind a building it is itself priced into. The one structural gate: the
*Rival* has no match-3 board, so it cannot manufacture — its second depot
needs grain + oil from its first depot's catchment diversity (or a future AI
trade/tribute pass). That matches the live-rival test's long-standing
expectation (exactly one depot until it earns oil) and is recorded here as a
follow-up, not a blocker: the human loop is proven unblocked above.
